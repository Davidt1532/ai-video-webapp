Scene 1: The Awakening
The old lighthouse keeper opens his eyes to a storm brewing over the Atlantic. Waves crash against the rocks below as lightning illuminates the sky. He sits up in his creaking chair, rubbing his tired eyes.

Scene 2: The Signal
A mysterious light appears on the horizon, pulsing in an odd rhythm. The keeper reaches for his dusty telescope, his hands trembling as he aims it toward the strange glow. It is not a ship.

Scene 3: The Message
Through the lens, he sees symbols carved into the light itself. Ancient runes he recognizes from the journal his grandfather left behind. The storm intensifies, but he cannot look away.

Scene 4: The Journal
He pulls the leather-bound journal from a locked drawer. Its pages are yellowed and brittle. The runes on the pages match exactly what he saw in the sky. His grandfather had written about this night, decades ago.
